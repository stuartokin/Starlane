How to use it

  1. Choose your settings below, or start from a preset. They save on this device as you go.
  2. ▶ Play a practice copy: opens the game with these settings on a copy of your commander. Leave test mode and everything goes back to how it was.
  3. ✅ Apply to my commander: changes the real commander saved on this device. The rules stay on until you remove them.
Applying the settings to a child's game in the maths-practice app

Open this page on the child's device (or send yourself the 🔗 link and open it there), unlock it and choose the settings. Then open Starlane from the maths-practice app, open test mode (triple-tap the score, or Ctrl+Shift+G, then the family passphrase), go to ✨ CHEATS and press ✅ Apply them to the REAL commander. 🧹 Remove custom rules on the same page puts the normal rules back.
